Jonathan Sauder is a postdoctoral fellow at MIT CSAIL in Sara Beery's lab, where he works at the intersection of machine learning, 3D computer vision, and ecology. Previosly, he obtained his PhD from EPFL in Lausanne, Switzerland, where his thesis project developed the next generation of tools for scaling up coral monitoring. Prior to that, Jonathan obtained his Master's in Computer Science from TU Berlin and his Bachelor's from the Hasso Plattner Institute at the University of Potsdam.
